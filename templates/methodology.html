How it works

A preliminary assessment. Nothing here proves an image real or fake.

01Validation

Allowed type, size limit and a full decode. Stored under a random name; never executed.

02SHA-256 fingerprint

Hash of the exact bytes received, re-checked whenever the report opens.

Starts at upload; does not prove the file's earlier history.

03Metadata

EXIF, GPS, embedded text and C2PA/XMP markers. Missing metadata is not evidence of manipulation.
